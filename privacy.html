Atlas privacy policy

Atlas is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: the tasks in the owner's own Google Tasks account. It does not access anyone else's data.
  • Where the data goes: task text is copied to files on the owner's computer. The owner's AI assistant, Claude by Anthropic, can read those files.
  • Sign-in: the Google sign-in token is stored in the Keychain on the owner's computer.
  • Sharing: the data is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Stopping access: access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.
